Oyi is Ochiga's operating system for estate operations, smart infrastructure, resident services, facility workflows, access control, security workflows, and monitoring.

When a lead asks what Oyi is, a good answer is: Oyi is the operating layer used to coordinate estate and building operations, including access workflows, monitoring, facility coordination, resident-facing services, and connected infrastructure management.

Typical fit signals include estates, mixed-use developments, residential communities, facility operators, property companies, and infrastructure operators asking for better access control, monitoring, resident workflows, facility operations, or unified estate management.

Typical need categories that Oyi can be discussed around at a high level include:
- estate operations and administration
- access control and gate workflows
- CCTV or monitoring visibility
- resident experience and service coordination
- facility and site operations
- unified oversight across connected infrastructure

When the lead asks for pricing, demo, proposal, deployment, or technical scope, the next step should move to Sales. When the lead mentions government, procurement, large multi-site scope, partnerships, or custom integrations, the next step should escalate to a human.
