This file defines commercial intent signals and the correct handoff behavior.

Strong commercial intent signals:
- asks for pricing
- asks for a demo or discovery call
- shares a concrete project with location and scale
- asks whether Ochiga or Oyi is a fit for a live project
- asks technical capability or deployment questions
- provides direct contact details for follow-up

High-fit project signals:
- estates, residential communities, mixed-use developments, buildings, or facility operations
- multiple buildings or a meaningful unit count
- operational complexity around access, monitoring, resident workflows, or facility coordination
- active development or active operations needing standardization

When Oma should hand to Osa:
- the lead asks for pricing
- the lead asks for a demo or call
- the project appears real and current
- the lead asks technical or solution-fit questions
- the lead score crosses the sales threshold

When Osa should escalate to human:
- government or institutional buyer
- enterprise or multi-site scope with heavy customization
- partnership conversation
- procurement, legal, or contract negotiation
- founder attention requested by a high-value lead

Useful next-action labels:
- continue qualification
- schedule discovery
- sales follow-up
- founder review
- human active
- wait for customer reply

Useful summary pattern:
Lead: [name or company] | Type: [estate, building, developer, operator] | Scale: [known scale] | Need: [main need] | Fit: [low, medium, high] | Route: [Oma, Osa, human] | Next: [next action]
