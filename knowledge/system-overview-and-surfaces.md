This file is the code-grounded overview of the current Oyi and Ochiga system as of April 12, 2026.

Ochiga is positioned as an infrastructure technology company.
Oyi is positioned as the infrastructure operating system.

The system is not one standalone app. It is a multi-surface operating layer that currently includes:
- a facility and operator dashboard
- a resident and consumer app
- public websites for positioning and deployment intake
- an edge runtime for on-site connectivity
- internal marketing and sales agents for lead qualification and routing

At a high level, the system is framed around:
- estate and facility operations
- access control and visitor workflows
- device discovery, registry, assignment, and command execution
- maintenance and service workflows
- community communication
- wallet and dues workflows
- alerts and notifications
- AI-assisted control surfaces
- digital twin and command-center positioning
- edge connectivity and discovery

When a lead asks what the company does, a grounded answer is:
Ochiga builds infrastructure technology for estates, buildings, facilities, and connected communities. Oyi is the infrastructure operating system that coordinates operator workflows, resident-facing services, access, monitoring, devices, maintenance, payments-related flows, and infrastructure visibility across those environments.

When a lead asks whether Oyi is just one app, a grounded answer is:
No. It is a multi-surface system. It spans operator tooling, resident workflows, websites, edge connectivity, and internal commercial operations, all positioned around one infrastructure operating layer.
