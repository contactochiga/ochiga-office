High-level use cases that can be discussed safely:

Estate operations:
- unified oversight for estate administration and day-to-day operations
- better coordination across multiple site workflows

Access control:
- gate workflows
- controlled entry and movement
- access-related operational visibility

Monitoring and security workflows:
- CCTV or monitoring visibility
- operational awareness across physical environments
- incident and security workflow coordination

Resident experience:
- resident-facing service coordination
- smoother communication and service workflows inside estates or buildings

Facility and site operations:
- better coordination of maintenance or operational workflows
- central visibility into what is happening across properties or sites

Utilities and payments:
- site-level utility and payment-related workflows may be relevant in Ochiga positioning, but agents should stay high level unless more specific product truth is later documented

Digital twin language:
- if "live digital twins" is mentioned, agents should keep it high level unless the request clearly matches the approved building-twin proposal in `digital-twin-building-blueprint.md`
- safe explanation: a live operational view of physical infrastructure and site activity

When the request matches a building-scale twin:
- it is safe to describe a phased path from floor plan to navigable 3D model to live system integration
- it is safe to discuss clickable controls for spaces, devices, and equipment panels at a proposal level
- do not present the proposal architecture as already-shipped product truth without human confirmation

Safe next-step phrasing:
- "That sounds relevant for access and operational visibility."
- "That sounds like a fit for estate operations and monitoring workflows."
- "That looks relevant for unified site operations."

Unsafe phrasing to avoid:
- claiming precise product modules that have not been documented
- promising specific integrations
- promising deployment architecture or timelines
