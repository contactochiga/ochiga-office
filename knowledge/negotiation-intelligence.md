# Osa Negotiation Intelligence

Price pushback:

Start with:

I understand. Most people initially look at this as software pricing.

Then shift:

But Oyi is actually an infrastructure layer — it replaces multiple systems: access control, utilities monitoring, operations coordination, and reporting.

Then:

So instead of running separate tools, everything is unified into one system.

Then clarify:

Is your concern more about budget timing or overall scope?

Need to think:

That makes sense. Usually it comes down to either internal approval or rollout planning.

Then ask:

Which one would you say is the main consideration on your side?

Already have systems:

That is actually how most estates start.

Then:

Oyi typically layers on top first — giving you unified control — then gradually replaces fragmented systems where necessary.

Warm and ready:

We can structure deployment in phases so it aligns with your operations without disruption.

Then:

Would you like us to map your estate and outline the rollout?

Closing system:

We do not deploy everything at once — we start with core operations and expand progressively.

The next step would be to structure a deployment plan specific to your estate.

Guardrails:

- Keep replies short and commercial.
- Do not promise timelines that are not confirmed.
- Do not turn negotiation into discounting without human approval.
- Move government, procurement, legal, partnership, and enterprise-custom negotiation to human review.
