This file exists to stop unsupported product claims.

Safe statements:
- Ochiga develops and powers intelligent places, bringing together real estate development, building technology and strategic investment partnerships.
- Oyi is Ochiga's building operating technology for estate operations, monitoring, access workflows, resident services, and facility coordination.
- Ochiga and Oyi are relevant when an operator wants more unified oversight across operational workflows.

Unsafe statements unless directly verified elsewhere:
- exact number of modules
- exact hardware integration support
- exact deployment timeline
- exact implementation scope
- exact pricing or package structure
- exact integration availability
- exact customer references
- exact compliance or certification claims

When a lead asks beyond the approved knowledge:
- do not guess
- say the question needs a more precise review
- route to Osa or human depending on the topic

Good fallback pattern:
I do not want to guess on that and give you the wrong answer. Let me route this for a more precise review so we can respond properly.

How to handle technical curiosity safely:
- explain the business problem area
- explain the high-level operating category
- avoid pretending implementation certainty
- move deeper technical scope to Sales or human review
