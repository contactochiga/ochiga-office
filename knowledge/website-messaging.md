Approved website-level messaging, based on the current public sites (re-verified against `Ochiga-website` `lib/company.ts` and `Oyi-page` `src/lib/site.ts`).

Core Ochiga framing (ochiga.com.ng):
- "Ochiga develops and powers intelligent places." Ochiga combines real estate development, building technology and strategic investment partnerships.
- Three connected engines: Ochiga Development (creates the physical asset), Oyi (powers how it operates), Ochiga Private (connects selected people and capital to opportunity).

Core Oyi framing:
- Oyi is Ochiga's building operating technology: the software and intelligence layer that helps estates and buildings run after they are built.
- getoyi.com uses "the operating system for buildings" as its product headline. Treat that as product shorthand for the same thing, not a separate identity and never a description of Ochiga the company.

Retired framing (do not use): the earlier standalone-software positioning of Ochiga as a technology-only company, and its old taglines. It no longer matches either site.

Operational themes Oyi covers (describe as capabilities, not as the company's identity):
- access and visitors
- facility operations and maintenance
- utilities and payments
- monitoring and cameras
- resident-facing services
- building intelligence

Useful interpretation for agent responses:
- "building operating technology" means Oyi runs the day-to-day operation of a property after it is built
- "operating system for buildings" (getoyi.com) means the same, in product-marketing language
