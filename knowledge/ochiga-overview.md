Ochiga develops and powers intelligent places through three connected engines: Ochiga Development (creates the physical asset), Oyi (powers how it operates) and Ochiga Private (connects selected people and capital to opportunity).

Oyi is Ochiga's building operating technology: the software and intelligence layer that helps estates and buildings run after they are built. The canonical definitions are Backend's `backend:corporate-company` and `backend:corporate-oyi`; this file does not redefine them.

"Building operating system" / "Operating System For Modern Buildings" is commercial shorthand for what Oyi does for a property. Use it in sales and proposal language if helpful, never as a replacement for the definition above, and never to describe Ochiga the company.

Oyi connects occupants, operators, infrastructure, services, security, utilities, devices, cameras, edge runtime, and intelligence into one platform. Do not describe Oyi narrowly as estate software, smart home software, visitor management software, CCTV software, or facility software. Those are capabilities inside Oyi.

Product structure (see Backend `backend:product-oyi-architecture`):
- Facility OS: the operator app for estates, buildings and services.
- Consumer OS: the resident app for a home.
- Oyi Edge: on-site software that connects local cameras and supported devices to the platform.
- Oyi Intelligence: the shared core behind the Oyi assistant: awareness, summaries, workflows and recommendations from permission-checked building data.

Commercial model:
Building Review / Site Visit comes first in early-market conversations, then Assessment, Deployment, Integration, Intelligence, and Subscription.

Packages (commercial packaging of Oyi, not separate products):
- Oyi Core: operational foundation.
- Oyi Operations: services, utilities, reporting, and daily management.
- Oyi Infrastructure: cameras, devices, access control readiness, edge runtime, and smart-building integrations.
- Oyi Command Center: multi-site operations, executive intelligence, portfolio command, digital twin readiness, and advanced workflows.
