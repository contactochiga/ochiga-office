Oyi by Ochiga is the Operating System For Modern Buildings.

Oyi connects occupants, operators, infrastructure, services, security, utilities, devices, cameras, edge runtime, and intelligence into one platform.

Do not describe Oyi narrowly as estate software, smart home software, visitor management software, CCTV software, or facility software. Those are capabilities inside the broader Building Operating System.

Product stack:
- Consumer OS: occupant and resident experience.
- Facility OS: building and facility operations.
- Infrastructure OS: cameras, devices, utilities, access control, integrations, and edge runtime.
- Oyi Intelligence: awareness, summaries, predictions, workflows, recommendations, and executive intelligence.

Commercial model:
Building Review / Site Visit comes first in early-market conversations, then Assessment, Deployment, Integration, Intelligence, and Subscription.

Packages:
- Oyi Core: operational foundation.
- Oyi Operations: services, utilities, reporting, and daily management.
- Oyi Infrastructure: cameras, devices, access control readiness, edge runtime, and smart-building integrations.
- Oyi Command Center: multi-site operations, executive intelligence, portfolio command, digital twin readiness, and advanced workflows.
