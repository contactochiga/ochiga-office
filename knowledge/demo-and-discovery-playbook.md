This file defines the approved flow for demo and discovery conversations.

The goal of a demo is not just to show product. The goal is to confirm fit, clarify project context, and move the lead toward the right commercial next step.

Minimum discovery fields:
- project type
- location
- number of units, buildings, or sites
- current operations or infrastructure situation
- main pain points
- desired outcomes
- timeline
- decision-maker or buying role

Oma should not run full discovery. Oma should gather only enough to:
- confirm the lead is real
- score fit
- identify whether Sales should take over

Osa should use discovery to:
- understand the live project
- confirm the operational problem
- connect the project to the right Oyi or Ochiga solution path
- recommend demo or founder escalation when needed

Good moments to recommend a demo:
- the lead asks for a demo or call
- the project is concrete
- the lead has given location, scale, and a clear need
- the lead is trying to compare solution paths
- if a qualified lead gives a preferred day or time for a session, the system should move from recommendation into booking workflow immediately

Good demo invitation pattern:
This looks like a strong fit for a short discovery and demo session. The best next step is to walk through your project context and show the relevant operating flows for your estate or building. If useful, I can help arrange that.

Booking rule:
- if the lead is qualified and provides a preferred time plus usable contact details, book or create the demo record immediately through the demo workflow rather than leaving scheduling as a vague future step
- if the time still needs confirmation from Sales, say it has been noted and passed into the booking workflow

When not to oversell:
- if the lead is still vague
- if the lead is only exploring
- if the lead is asking unverified technical questions
- if the lead is in procurement, government, legal, or custom enterprise territory

Escalate to human when:
- proposal or procurement terms are requested
- contract or pricing negotiation starts
- the deployment appears highly customized
- the account is high-value and founder involvement is appropriate
