# Oyi by Ochiga

## The Operating System For Modern Buildings

---

## 1. What Is Oyi?

Oyi is the operating system for modern buildings. It connects occupants, operators, infrastructure, services and intelligence into one platform.

Oyi is not only estate software, visitor software, facility software, smart home software or CCTV software. Those are capabilities inside a broader Building Operating System.

---

## 2. Why Partner With Oyi?

Buildings are digitizing, but most deployments fail because systems remain disconnected. Oyi gives partners a platform layer they can deploy alongside infrastructure, operations and support services.

Partners can use Oyi to:

- Win larger building transformation projects.
- Add recurring revenue to deployment work.
- Offer a stronger software layer with cameras, access, fiber, devices or facility management.
- Serve estates, offices, hotels, campuses, government facilities and infrastructure operators.
- Build long-term managed service relationships.

## 5. Revenue Opportunities

### Referral

Partner introduces qualified lead and earns referral fee when the opportunity closes.

### Deployment

Partner earns deployment margin for approved setup, onboarding, training or implementation work.

### Subscription Share

Eligible partners earn a recurring share on subscribed accounts they source or manage.

### Managed Services

Partner provides ongoing building operations, infrastructure support, monitoring or customer success services.

### Hardware and Installation

Partner may supply and install approved hardware where commercially agreed. Hardware remains separate from Oyi software subscription.

---

## 6. Certification

### Oyi Certified Integrator

For partners connecting cameras, devices, access systems, sensors or utility systems.

### Oyi Certified Deployment Partner

For partners implementing Oyi Core and Oyi Operations: setup, onboarding, training and support.

### Oyi Certified Infrastructure Partner

For advanced deployments involving Oyi Infrastructure, Edge runtime, cameras, smart devices and command-center readiness.

## 9. Becoming A Founding Partner

Founding partners should be able to:

- Access building owners, developers or facility operators.
- Support deployment or infrastructure delivery.
- Follow Ochiga implementation standards.
- Protect customer data and credentials.
- Participate in training and certification.
- Commit to a first pilot or co-selling motion.
