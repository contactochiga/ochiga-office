## 1. Discovery Script

### Opening

Most buildings today are operated through disconnected tools: WhatsApp, spreadsheets, cameras, radios, paper logs and separate utility systems. Oyi brings occupants, operators, infrastructure and intelligence into one Building Operating System.

### Framing Question

Can I understand how your building currently manages visitors, maintenance, communication, utilities and security operations?

### Building Questions

- What type of property do you operate?
- How many buildings, units, rooms, tenants, occupants or spaces are involved?
- Who manages the facility daily?
- Who makes technology or operational decisions?
- Is this for one site or multiple sites?

### Operations Questions

- How do occupants currently communicate with management?
- How do you manage visitor access?
- How do maintenance requests get reported and tracked?
- How do you notify residents, tenants or occupants?
- What is the most frustrating operational issue today?

### Infrastructure Questions

- Do you have existing CCTV cameras, DVRs or NVRs?
- Do you have access control?
- Do you have smart meters, utility systems, smart devices or sensors?
- Is there reliable internet or local network access?
- Who maintains the infrastructure today?

### Commercial Questions

- Is there an immediate project or future planning need?
- What timeline are you considering?
- Is there an approved budget or budget range?
- What would make this successful in the first 30 days?

### Close

The best next step is a Building Review. We map your current operations, infrastructure readiness and deployment path, then recommend the right Oyi package and rollout plan.

## 3. Objection Handling Guide

### "We already use WhatsApp."

WhatsApp is communication. Oyi is operations. Oyi gives you records, workflows, visibility, accountability and intelligence across the building.

### "We already have cameras."

Perfect. Oyi is not trying to replace cameras. Oyi can help connect camera operations, events and building workflows where the infrastructure supports it.

### "Residents may not adopt it."

Residents adopt utility. Visitors, maintenance, payments, services and important updates are daily reasons to use Oyi.

### "Is this just AI?"

No. AI is one layer. The core product is a Building Operating System: Consumer OS, Facility OS, Infrastructure OS and Oyi Intelligence.

### "Can it scale?"

Yes. Oyi is designed for single buildings, estates, campuses and future multi-site command operations.

### "We are not ready for cameras/devices yet."

Start with Oyi Core or Oyi Operations. Infrastructure can be added after the foundation is live.

### "How much does it cost?"

Pricing depends on the package and deployment scope. We usually start with a Building Review, then recommend Core, Operations, Infrastructure or Command Center.
