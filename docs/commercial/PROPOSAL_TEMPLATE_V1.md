## 6. Integration Scope

Integration scope depends on site readiness, supported equipment, credentials and network access.

Potential integrations:

- Cameras and DVR/NVR channels.

## 7. Intelligence Scope

Oyi Intelligence will support:

- Home/building awareness.
- Activity summaries.
- Maintenance and visitor awareness.
- Device/runtime awareness where integrated.
- Prediction and workflow summaries where available.
- Executive/Facility summaries for command-level visibility.

Oyi Intelligence uses real operational data. It should not claim certainty where evidence is incomplete.
