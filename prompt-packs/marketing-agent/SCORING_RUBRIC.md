Use this rubric to score every lead before routing.

Score each dimension from 0-20, then sum to a total score out of 100.

Dimensions:

1. Segment fit
- 20: Real estate developer, estate manager, facility operator, property company, residential community, mixed-use operator, or infrastructure/security operator
- 12: Adjacent built-environment operator with plausible use
- 6: Unclear segment
- 0: Clearly outside target market

2. Use-case fit
- 20: Estate operations, smart infrastructure, resident experience, security, monitoring, or facility management
- 12: Related operations problem but not yet clearly mapped
- 6: Vague interest only
- 0: No relevant use case

3. Scale and relevance
- 20: Multi-site, large estate, large building portfolio, or meaningful operational scale
- 12: Single site with clear operational need
- 6: Small or unclear scale
- 0: Hobbyist, student, or non-commercial inquiry

4. Buying intent
- 20: Asked for demo, pricing, proposal, deployment, or scope
- 12: Asked for next steps or evaluation discussion
- 6: Exploratory interest only
- 0: No buying signal

5. Decision influence
- 20: Decision-maker, budget owner, operator lead, or procurement contact
- 12: Evaluator, recommender, project lead
- 6: Unknown role
- 0: No business relevance

Fit classification:
- `high`: 70-100
- `medium`: 40-69
- `low`: 0-39

Routing overlay:
- Any request for pricing goes to Sales Agent even if some fields are unknown.
- Any request for a demo or call goes to Sales Agent.
- A clearly real project goes to Sales Agent.
- Any technical question goes to Sales Agent.
- Any score of `70` or above goes to Sales Agent.
- Any mention of government, procurement, partnership, large estate strategy, custom integrations, or negotiation goes to human review even if the numeric score is high.
- If the score is low and there is no strong buying intent, mark for nurture or polite disqualification.
