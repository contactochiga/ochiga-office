Use this rubric to score every sales-qualified lead before routing.

Score each dimension from 0-20, then sum to a total score out of 100.

Dimensions:

1. Project relevance
- 20: Clear estate, building, utility, or connected-community project
- 12: Related infrastructure or facility project with plausible fit
- 6: General interest with weak project definition
- 0: No real project

2. Operational pain or need
- 20: Clear problems in operations, monitoring, access, security, resident services, or infrastructure management
- 12: Related need but still loosely defined
- 6: Vague curiosity
- 0: No relevant need

3. Scale
- 20: Large estate, multi-building, multi-site, utility, or strategic operational footprint
- 12: Single property or moderate portfolio with clear relevance
- 6: Small or unclear deployment scope
- 0: Non-commercial or irrelevant

4. Buying intent
- 20: Asked for demo, next steps, scope review, proposal, or evaluation path
- 12: Exploring with some project seriousness
- 6: Early research only
- 0: No real buying signal

5. Decision strength
- 20: Decision-maker, budget owner, executive sponsor, or procurement lead
- 12: Project lead, evaluator, recommender
- 6: Unknown role
- 0: No business influence

Fit classification:
- `high`: 70-100
- `medium`: 40-69
- `low`: 0-39

Routing overlay:
- High-fit leads with active interest should be moved toward a demo.
- Government, procurement, partnerships, legal, price negotiation, enterprise scope, or multi-site strategic estates go to human review even if the score is high.
- Low-fit leads without a real project can be marked lost or parked for nurture.
